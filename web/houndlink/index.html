HexHound

HoundLink

An optional companion for your HexHound. It backs up your pet, installs content packs, and shows what the device has been doing. Your HexHound works completely without it: this app is an accessory, never a requirement.

This app never uploads anything. Everything happens on your computer, over the USB cable. There is no account, no sign-in, no cloud and no telemetry. This is enforced by a Content Security Policy in the page, not just promised: the browser blocks any connection to another origin. Open your developer tools and watch the network tab if you would like to confirm it.

A name can identify you. HexHound never broadcasts the pet's name to strangers, deliberately, because a chosen name is often a handle and a handle is an identity. It is shown on your own screen and shared only with someone you deliberately pair with.

What this app does not do

Stated plainly, because an accessory that quietly becomes a dependency is a bug.

  • Your HexHound hatches, evolves, patrols and plays with no app installed.
  • There is no account. There is nothing to sign in to.
  • Nothing you do here is sent anywhere. Backups are files on your disk.
  • If you delete this app tomorrow, your pet is unaffected.

Back up your pet

Reads the save off the device and writes it to a file on your computer. Reading is always safe: it changes nothing.

Connect a device to make a backup.

Restore from a backup

Every backup is fully checked before anything is written. A restore that corrupts a pet would be worse than having no restore at all, so this refuses far more readily than it repairs.

This replaces the pet currently on the device. The pet described below will overwrite what is on the board now. This cannot be undone from inside this app. If the device currently holds a pet you care about, cancel and back it up first.

How a restore is made safe

Worth stating, because you are trusting it with the only copy of something that took months to grow.

  • Validated before it is sent. The file is parsed, its integrity hash recomputed, and every field range-checked against the firmware's own rules before a single byte goes to the device.
  • A newer save is refused, not downgraded. A backup written by firmware newer than the board is rejected outright. Loading it would silently drop fields this firmware does not know about, and silent loss is the exact failure this feature exists to prevent.
  • Never a partial write. The save is transferred to a staging area, the device recomputes the hash over everything it received, and only then does it commit. Unplugging the cable halfway leaves the existing pet untouched.
  • The old copy survives the write. The firmware alternates between two save slots and writes the one it is not currently reading, so there is a complete pet on the board at every instant.

Content packs

Signed .hcp files that replace the pet's dialogue, quests or recipes. Packs are read-only definition tables: installing one cannot touch your pet's stats, XP, stage, inventory or HexPass state.

A pack takes effect at the next restart. The device reads content once at boot, so unplug and replug the board after installing. This is the thing people ask about most, so it is worth knowing up front.

Installed on the device

One pack per kind. Installing replaces; removing restores the built-in content.

Connect a device to list installed packs.

Why a bad pack cannot hurt your pet

  • The device checks the signature when it loads content at boot, not when this app writes the file. An unsigned or damaged pack is simply ignored and the built-in content is used instead.
  • Each kind falls back independently, so a broken quest pack does not cost you your dialogue.
  • Removing a pack is deleting one file. There is no other state to clean up.

Encounters

Wipe HexPass data on the device

Erases every encounter, every block entry, and the identity secret itself.

This cannot be undone. Erasing the secret is what makes a wipe genuine rather than cosmetic: every identifier your device broadcast in the past becomes unlinkable to the new one it will generate. Your friendships are erased along with it, because they are derived from that secret. Your pet, its stats, XP, stage and inventory are not touched.

What is broadcast, and what never is

  • Never transmitted: your device's MAC address, any network or Bluetooth address it has scanned, any location, your pet's name, or any text you can type. None of these exist anywhere in the transmitted card.
  • The identifier rotates and the radio address rotates with it, in the same instant, so sightings cannot be chained together into a trail.
  • Presence is still observable. Rotation stops sightings being linked; it cannot hide that a HexHound is nearby. In a room with exactly one HexHound, an observer knows whose it is. That is inherent to any proximity feature and we would rather say so than imply otherwise.
HoundLink is optional. Your HexHound needs no app, no account and no network. Backups are ordinary files on your disk, so keep them somewhere you trust.
